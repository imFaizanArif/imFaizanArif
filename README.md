# Hi, Earth! 🌎 I'm Faizan Arif 👋

### 🚀 About Me

I'm a **Software Engineer** who makes computers understand *where* things are. 📍

I spend my days with **LiDAR point clouds, BIM models, Gaussian splats and live sensor data**, getting them to line up in one 3D scene and make sense in a browser. Currently working at **Kodifly** as a **Software Engineer III**, building Digital Twins for infrastructure monitoring in Hong Kong. 🏗️

- 🤝 Open to **research collaborations** and **remote roles** around geospatial, 3D and Digital Twin work.
